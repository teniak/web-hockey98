Born between the boards and the amps

Hockey 98 is a small Swiss brand built on two loves: the ice and loud guitars.

Every design is drawn by hand, inspired by Swiss hockey culture and the rock and tattoo art we grew up with. No stock graphics, no templates — just ink, attitude and a lot of hours at the rink.
